Raw Footage FAQ

What does raw footage include?

Raw footage is captured aerial clips without a full edited video. Delivery details depend on the project and file size.

Is editing included?

No. Raw footage is capture-only. If you need an edited video, we can quote that separately.

Who is raw footage best for?

It is useful for agencies, editors, businesses, production crews, and teams that already have an edit plan.

Can you follow a shot list?

Yes. Send the angles, movement, location notes, and clip types before the shoot.

How are files delivered?

Files are delivered digitally. Larger projects may need extra upload time depending on file size and delivery format.
