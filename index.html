Why Work With Me

I'm Denny, and I've been capturing Las Vegas from every angle since long before I had a drone in the air. I shoot ground photos, capture aerials when they help, and bring over 10 years of photo and video editing experience to the final delivery.

Since earning my Part 107 in 2025, I've combined technical flying skills with a decade of post-production experience to deliver polished, professional work across real estate, business marketing, construction updates, and raw footage capture. I'm based in Las Vegas (since 1998) and know the valley inside and out, especially the West Side from southwest to northwest.
