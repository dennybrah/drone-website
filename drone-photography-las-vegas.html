Drone & Aerial FAQ

What areas do you serve?

LV Logic Media serves Las Vegas and nearby areas. Travel or remote locations can be quoted before booking.

Can drone work be done near airports?

Sometimes. Airspace, location, weather, and timing are checked before confirming a flight.

What affects drone shoot pricing?

Pricing depends on location, access, flight time, shot list, deliverables, and whether you need editing or raw footage.

Do you provide edited video or raw footage?

Both are available. We can deliver edited aerial video or clean raw drone footage for your editor or team.

Are you certified for commercial drone work?

Yes. Flights are handled by an FAA Part 107 certified Las Vegas drone photographer.
