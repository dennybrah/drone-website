Construction Drone FAQ

How often can updates be scheduled?

Monthly updates are common, but biweekly, weekly, or milestone-based visits can be quoted if the project needs them.

What does a construction update include?

Updates can include aerial photos, short clips, site context, staging areas, access, and progress views based on the shot list.

Can you shoot the same angles over time?

Yes. Repeatable angles are useful for comparing progress across multiple site visits.
